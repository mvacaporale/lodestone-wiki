# Lodestone

The public slice of my personal knowledge vault — 30 notes on systems thinking, AI alignment, complexity science, and craft, published 2026-10-06.

## Why "Lodestone"

A lodestone is a naturally magnetized stone — the mineral early navigators rubbed their needles against to make the first compasses. My vault is named for the same job: a fixed point that holds orientation, that thinking can be checked against. This wiki is the part of it that points outward.

## How to read this

- **[Start at the wiki index](wiki/index.md)** — LLM-organized topic pages that map the notes and suggest reading orders.
- **Browse [notes/](notes/)** — the notes themselves, my own writing, verbatim.
- **Ask an LLM** — paste the raw [`llms-full.txt`](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mvacaporale/lodestone-wiki/main/llms-full.txt) URL into Claude, ChatGPT, or any assistant and ask it questions about all of this at once.

## Two voices, never blended

Everything under `notes/` is **my writing** — published verbatim from the vault, except for privacy redactions. Everything under `wiki/` is **LLM-generated organization** — topic pages, summaries, and cross-links built from the notes at publish time, and labeled as such. You always know which voice you're hearing.

Notes are permanent; wiki pages are regenerated on each publish and may change.

## How this is made

A note in my vault is private unless I explicitly flag it for publication; an LLM curator nominates candidates weekly, I approve, and a build script copies approved notes through a leak linter into this repo. The full system — including how to replicate it — is documented in [How This Wiki Was Built](notes/how-this-wiki-was-built.md).

Also from the same vault: a [weekly digest](https://github.com/mvacaporale/weekly-digest) with an RSS feed.
