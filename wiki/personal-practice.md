# Personal Practice

The human-scale cluster: how to relate to people, help them grow, and get meaningful work done. These notes are less a single argument than a toolkit, but they share a sensibility with the rest of the collection — attention to what a process actually optimizes for. Coaching works by drawing out the client's own wisdom rather than prescribing answers; good conversation works by finding what someone genuinely cares about; good prioritization works by picking the one task with leverage rather than maximizing volume.

Two research syntheses anchor the cluster. The coaching note distills best practices — powerful questions, advice given sparingly and with permission, perspective-challenging frameworks like GROW and cognitive reframing — explicitly framed as design guidance for building an AI coach, which connects it back to [Building with AI](building-with-ai.md). The project-planning synthesis surveys classical frameworks (GTD, PARA, Agile, CPM) and the research on why plans fail, distilling them into transferable principles. The shorter notes are working references the author actually uses: questions for meeting people, a morning prioritization ritual, and a compact statement of cognitive load theory for anyone trying to learn or teach.
