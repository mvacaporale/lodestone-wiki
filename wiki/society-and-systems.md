# Society and Systems

Where [The Alignment Arc](alignment-as-coupling.md) builds the theory, this cluster names the problem it was built for. Michaelangelo's diagnosis, repeated across these notes, is that society's fundamental failure is **misaligned optimization**: our systems — media, markets, schools, healthcare — optimize proxies (profit, attention, test scores, procedures) rather than the things we actually value (wellbeing, truth, connection, flourishing). In his phrase, "we're optimizing the wrong variables at scale — and doing it very efficiently." Layered under that is a sensemaking crisis: fragmented epistemics mean we can't even agree on what's happening, which blocks coordinated repair. AI, in this picture, is an amplifier that could entrench the dysfunction or power a conscious realignment, depending on what it's pointed at.

The cluster also holds his sketches of what better might look like. One thread looks backward: hunter-gatherer "primitive affluence" as evidence that scarcity-feeling is substantially manufactured by comparison and hierarchy, not material lack. Another looks forward: a society under AI abundance organized around ~20-hour weeks of community contribution, with the relational core (teachers, therapists) kept human. A third looks sideways, at scale: Mitra Martin's case that flourishing is built at the level of the neighborhood — the intergenerational container — rather than the individual or the state.

Start with the [Society and Systems MOC](../notes/society-and-systems-moc.md), which is the author's own map of this territory, then follow what pulls you.
