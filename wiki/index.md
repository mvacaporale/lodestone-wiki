# Lodestone Wiki — The Map

This is the published slice of Michaelangelo Caporale's personal knowledge base. The [notes](../notes/) are his own writing — syntheses, research digests, voice-memo distillations, and working questions — published from a private Obsidian vault via the pipeline he describes in [How This Wiki Was Built](../notes/how-this-wiki-was-built.md). This wiki layer is the machine-generated shelf that organizes them.

The center of gravity is a single question seen from several angles: **why do systems made of well-meaning parts — cells, people, institutions, AIs — fall out of alignment with what their members actually value, and what would it take to re-align them?** Around that core sit more practical collections on building with AI and on personal craft.

## The clusters

- **[The Alignment Arc](alignment-as-coupling.md)** — A multi-part argument that alignment (biological, social, and AI) is a story about coupling: shared fate, honest signals, and how a "we" becomes an "I." The intellectual spine of the collection.
- **[Society and Systems](society-and-systems.md)** — Society's core failure as misaligned optimization: systems chasing proxies (profit, attention, test scores) instead of flourishing — and sketches of what better might look like, from AI-abundance economics to intergenerational neighborhoods.
- **[How Systems Get Stuck — and Change](systems-change.md)** — The mechanics underneath both clusters above: attractor basins, energy sinks, Goodhart traps, and the cross-scale conditions under which self-organizing systems actually update their models of reality.
- **[Building with AI](building-with-ai.md)** — Practical craft for working with AI tools: backend stacks for coding agents, prompting strategies, AI-assisted design, product development process, and the knowledge system this wiki itself runs on.
- **[Personal Practice](personal-practice.md)** — The human-scale toolkit: coaching, questions that open real conversation, prioritization, project planning, and how learning works.

## Where to start

Start with **[The Alignment Arc](alignment-as-coupling.md)** — it's the most developed thread and the lens the rest of the collection looks through. If you want the problem before the theory, start with **[Society and Systems](society-and-systems.md)**. For the meta-story of how this site exists at all, read [How This Wiki Was Built](../notes/how-this-wiki-was-built.md).
