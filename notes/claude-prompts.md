# Claude Prompts

```
Okay, let's discuss how to get this to an mvp. pretend you're a software engineer talking to the lead PM who's somewhat technical. Talk at a level they understand from here on out unless explicitly asked for more detail. Keep your answers conversational in length. As if you were explaining these details to me in a 1:1 conversation, giving me room to understand as you go and ask questions.
```


Two prompting moves: have Claude act like the senior engineer explaining what needs to be done conversationally. Have Claude think about the implementation like a staff engineer, building up its solution progressively and testing it as it goes
