## Part I — Why AI Thinks AI Is Bad

**Slide 5 — A Conversation with ChatGPT**
- I asked the models for society's root problem; they converge on the same answer: we build systems that **optimize proxies** (money, clicks, votes) faster than we can tie those proxies to what we value.
- That gap — proxy racing ahead of value — is Goodhart's law at civilizational scale.
- Note the models are describing *our* failure, not a future robot's.

**Slide 6 — Reframing the AI Alignment Problem**
- Standard question: *will the AI do what its designers intended?* (control — align the agent).
- The deeper question: *what happens when AI inherits the objective landscape we've already built?* (a misaligned world doesn't need a misaligned AI to go wrong).
- Punchline: **you can't align an agent in isolation from its system** — which is why the rest of the talk is about systems, not agents.

## Part II — A Sketch on Alignment

**Slide 8 — A Lesson From Evolution**
- Major transitions (Maynard Smith & Szathmáry): things that used to replicate on their own became *parts* of a larger whole — genes→chromosomes, cells→organisms, individuals→colonies.
- The enabling condition wasn't enforced agreement — it was **shared fate**: coordination scaled when a part's fate got genuinely tied to the whole's.
- Order of causation matters: fate gets coupled *first*, then the policing/suppression machinery evolves to protect it. (Increasing coordination ← greater shared fate.)

**Slide 9 — Alignment Is a Story About Coupling**
- Coupling = a **shared, consequence-carrying signal** every part reads and writes, tied to the whole's fate.
- Picture the cell: it only ever responds to *local* readings — pH, ATP, oxygen — yet those locals are physically tied to the body's state.
- So "keep myself in balance" *is* "keep my piece of the body in balance." Doing well locally = helping the whole. No altruism required.

**Slide 10 — Candidate Definition of Alignment**
- **Def: a system is aligned when its parts and its whole can only descend their free-energy gradients together.** ("Free energy" ≈ how far you are from your expected/healthy state — everything is just trying to reduce it.)
- It sounds like a tautology ("parts serve the whole") — the content is in *only together*: an aligned part has **no private gradient it can defect down**.
- When the local signal stays a faithful summary of the whole's fate → honest signal. When it stops → Goodhart, externality, cancer.

**Slide 11 — Implications on This Definition**
- **You can't align an agent in isolation** — alignment is a property of the part *and* its coupling, not the part alone.
- **Stacked alignment = recursively applying the principle** — honest summary up, real setpoint down, at every level; the *weakest rung* governs the whole stack.
- **Health = maintaining the desired state**; **externalities = the shared signal dropped fate-relevant information** (the wire started lying).

if alignment lives in the coupling, you buy it partly by engineering the shared signal / environment,

## Part III — Defining the Goal of the System

**Slide 13 — Degenerate Cases: Align to What?**
- The definition is **values-agnostic** — it's a mechanism, not a purpose.
- Body scale: you make and destroy ~100B immune cells a day. Perfect alignment — and it does *not* mean the system cares about the part. (Alignment ≠ flourishing of the parts.)
- Earth scale: a global "ice age" homeostasis could be perfectly aligned and perfectly dull. Minimizing free energy is not the same as living well — **the goal has to be added; biology won't hand it to us** (the normativity gap).

**Slide 14 — We Can Create a Landscape, But What Shape?**
- Once you can build honest coupling, you can *reshape the objective landscape* on purpose.
- So the question flips from "how do we align?" to **"align to what?"** — we're choosing setpoints, not discovering them.
- This is the real design problem, and it's a values problem, not a math problem.

**Slide 15 — Leverage Points (Donella Meadows)**
- #6 information flows = the **shared signal itself**; most "fixes" tweak parameters, but repairing the wire is where leverage starts.
- The top of her list — #3 goals, #2 paradigm, #1 power to transcend paradigms — is exactly the "align to what" question.
- The move: use #6 (better disconfirming evidence) to force a #2 paradigm shift — and stay humble, hold certainty loosely.

**Slide 16 — Ultimate Goal: Alignment to the Biosphere**
- A candidate top setpoint: the **biosphere** — the widest whole whose fate we genuinely, physically share.
- It's a proposal, not a proof — but it's the level where "shared fate" is literally true rather than asserted.
- Open questions: formalizing + verifying a general theory of alignment; where AI tools could help build/defend the honest signals.

### Raw source (kept for reference)

*Evolution, full version:* Maynard Smith & Szathmáry's major transitions identify points where independent replicators became parts of a larger unit that replicates as a whole. They framed the central problem as conflict suppression. West and colleagues argued causation runs the other way — transitions begin where reproductive fate is already shared, and suppression mechanisms evolve afterward to maintain what's already coherent.

*Degenerate cases, full version:* neutrophils — body makes ~100B/day, self-destruct on a fixed cadence; gut lining replaced every 3–5 days. Ice-age homeostasis: humans indoors, low activity; free energy minimized in an aligned system, but subjective experience dull. Scale is also subjective.
