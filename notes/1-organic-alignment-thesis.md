# Organic Alignment — The Thesis

## Softmax & organic alignment

- Emmett Shear's lab (with Adam Goldstein and David Bloomin) argues alignment should be **grown, not imposed**.
- The core image: *"the 'we' of the cells becomes an 'I'"* — when many small agents cooperate tightly enough, a genuinely new agent emerges at the higher level, with goals none of the parts have.
- The sharpest critique (Wei Dai): humans are *already* organically aligned, and it produces Stalins — and a bigger "whole" might reclassify humans as expendable parts.

## Alignment as a process

- the agents that stay aligned with us are the ones that stay aligned with each other
- ==cooperation becomes an intrinsic skill rather than an imposed rule==
- Control has a scaling problem built in
- Every quadrant is bad except one: a being that genuinely cares.
- Alignment is a process, not a state.
- Alignment is alignment to something." It has to be continuously re-knit, like a family.
- capability and alignment co-scale — a smarter agent gets better at aligning itself, because cooperation is a skill intelligence sharpens.
