# Claude - Hunter-Gatherers and Affluence

**Added:** February 18, 2026 at 12:09 AM

---

## Primitive Affluence (Sahlins)

- Hunter-gatherers achieved what Sahlins called "primitive affluence" — not by having more, but by wanting less.
- We're culturally wired to always want more.
- The American Dream essentially celebrates the endless hustle to narrow that gap.

## The Ju/'hoansi Model (Suzman)

- The hunter-gatherer model of affluence depended on nobody being substantially richer or more powerful than anyone else.
- Modern consumer culture and constant comparison (amplified by social media) creates a perpetual sense of insufficiency.
- Suzman notes that even when basic needs are met, people feel deeply dissatisfied when they perceive the distribution of resources as grossly unfair.
- The Ju/'hoansi evidence suggests people are perfectly capable of leading fulfilled lives not defined by labor.
- They trusted their environment to provide and didn't obsess about the past or future.

## The Neolithic Revolution Changed Everything

When farming arrived, everything shifted:

1. **Surpluses became possible** for the first time — and they became "tangible rewards for labor," creating the novel idea that you earned and deserved more than others.
2. **Storing food became equivalent to "storing labor,"** which became the abstract concept of wealth.
3. **Surplus required management,** which created role differentiation — priests, warriors, artisans, accountants — and suddenly not everyone was equal.
4. **Control over the distribution of surpluses became power,** and surpluses were transformed into debt, money, and social hierarchy.

## Jealousy as Social Mechanism

- Their social system channeled jealousy into an equalizing force. It was the "invisible hand" of their economy.
- The fundamental driver isn't "I want more stuff" — it's "I want fairness."

## Root Cause According to Suzman

If I were to name the root cause: the compulsive quality of wanting more, the part that makes it feel unchecked, is coming from the comparison side.
