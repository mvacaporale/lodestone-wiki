# Good Personal Questions

Running reference of questions that open up conversation. Use as a menu when meeting someone new or going deeper with people I know.

## Casual openers

- What do you care about at the moment?
- What has the week brought you?

## Going deeper

- What's the best social container you've experienced?
- What are some things you think everyone should experience at some point in their life?
- What books / sci-fi have shaped your thinking?
- Something you're excited about, something you've changed your mind about, something that excited you as a kid *(captured 2026-07-28)*
- What do you think other people should be thinking about more? *(captured 2026-08-30)*
- What's essential to know about you? *(captured 2026-08-30)*
- What did you love to do as a kid? *(captured 2026-09-27)*
- What's something you want to do more of this year? *(captured 2026-09-27)*
