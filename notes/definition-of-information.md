# A Fundamental Definition of Information

## Core Definition

**Information is the structure that constrains the possible states of a system.**

The more a system's possible states are constrained, the more information it contains. This works at every level of reality — physical, biological, cognitive, and social — and does not require an observer or meaning to exist.

---

## Key Corollaries

- **Shannon's formulation**: Information is the reduction of uncertainty. Mathematically equivalent to the above — more constraints = less uncertainty = more information encoded.
- **Bateson's formulation**: "A difference that makes a difference." Information exists in the *relationship* between systems, not just within a single system.
- **Meaning as a special case**: Meaning emerges when one constrained system (a mind) interprets the constraints of another. It is not a property of information itself, but of its relationship to a conscious interpreter.

---

## Concrete Examples

### Physical Reality
A crystal lattice constrains the positions of its atoms to a highly ordered structure. It contains more information than a gas, whose molecular positions are largely unconstrained. The information exists independently of any observer.

### DNA
A strand of DNA constrains which proteins a cell can produce. The sequence of base pairs is a highly specific set of constraints — change one base pair and you change the output. Information here is physical, causal, and observer-independent.

### Ecological Systems
A flower's color and shape constrain which insects are likely to interact with it. A pheromone trail constrains the movement of an ant colony. Information flows through ecosystems as patterns in one system that causally influence another.

### A Chair
A chair's geometry constrains possible physical interactions — you can sit on it, not walk through it. But more deeply, the chair crystallizes centuries of accumulated human problem-solving: material science, ergonomics, manufacturing technique. This accumulated knowledge — what César Hidalgo calls "embodied knowledge" in *How Information Grows* — is distributed between the object itself and the neural pathways of the humans who conceived and built it.

### An LLM
A large language model is a compressed probability distribution over language. Its billions of parameters constrain what outputs are possible given any input — it cannot respond randomly, only in ways consistent with its learned structure. It carries vast information precisely because its possible states are so tightly constrained by its training.

### Spoken Language
Sound waves carry a physical signal, but spoken language only becomes *meaningful* information through a shared interpretive system between speaker and listener. The constraints exist at two levels: the physical signal, and the cultural/cognitive system that interprets it.

### Money
Money is a social coordination mechanism — not "true" or "false," but a shared constraint on behavior. Its value is information in the sense that it constrains what people will do, what they will accept, how they will cooperate. This is Harari's domain: information as social infrastructure.

## A Hierarchy of Information Types

| Type | Example | Observer Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Physical pattern | Crystal structure, gravity | No |
| Biological encoding | DNA, neural pathways | No |
| Embodied knowledge | A chair, a tool, a city | No (but created by observers) |
| Transmitted signal | Sound waves, light | No |
| Meaning | Language, symbols | Yes |
| Social coordination | Money, laws, myths | Yes (shared) |

---

## Relation to Harari's Framework

Harari's definition of information — signals that coordinate behavior between conscious agents — is a narrow subset of this broader definition. It operates at the **meaning** and **social coordination** levels. His insight is that at this level, the connective function of information (does it build networks?) often dominates its truth-representing function (does it accurately describe reality?).

The broader definition helps clarify why this is a *special case*, not the whole story.
