# Framework - Energy Sinks

**The framework: annealing applied to social systems** Borrowed from neuroscience: a rigid system is stuck in a deep _attractor_. Raise the system's _energy_ and it can escape that valley and settle into a healthier one. Strategic move: build _illegible energy sources_ — something the system's sensors can't read, so it can't tax, game, or co-opt it — and stay illegible until you're a strong enough attractor to survive contact. Nuance: _valence gradient_ tells it which way is "healthier"

**Where the main sinks lie**

- _Abstract taxonomy:_ complexity upkeep (Tainter), proxy-gaming (Goodhart), immune response / co-option, and legibility overhead (Scott).
- _The master sink in education:_ the admissions metric itself — a context-free comparability token (the standardized test above all) that the whole K-12 system routes its energy into producing.
- _The lock:_ a coordination equilibrium — neither high schools nor colleges can defect alone.
- _The sinks behind the gate:_ US News rankings, the prestige market, the employer-signaling chain, post-SFFA legal defensibility, and throughput/compression cost. The gate is pushed from behind, too.

**Nuances learned**

- The sink is _blind to student's personal growth by construction_ — comparability and value-add are orthogonal
- The test is sticky not as a ranking input (~5%) but because it sits _upstream_ of all six comparability functions at once.
- "Outcomes" metrics are mostly _selectivity laundering_ — rankings track a latent prestige variable, which is why overhauling the formula left the hierarchy unmoved.
- _Both_ sides are trapped by higher-order equilibria — the hoped-for "first-defector college" is itself chained.
- _Co-option is the live failure mode:_ the most successful escape (MTC) got bought by ETS — the escape hatch moved inside the building.
- Legibility is double-edged: poison toward the incumbent, but _internal_ legibility is how dispersed energy coordinates into a bloc.
