# Measuring Emergence — Causal Emergence & the Free Energy Principle

The companion to [6 - The Coupling Spine - From the Formal Model to the Authored Top](6-the-coupling-spine-from-the-formal-model-to-the-authored-top.md). Where Note 6 says what coupling *is*, this note supplies the **instruments**: how you detect that a higher self has actually formed, and how you tell whether it is viable. Two measures, answering *when* and *what-next*.

> Primary sources to pull: Erik Hoel on causal emergence; Karl Friston / active inference for the FEP.

## Causal emergence (Erik Hoel) — the *when*

- The claim: sometimes a coarse-grained **macro** description has *more* causal power than the fine-grained **micro** one. When that happens, ==causation has "moved up a level."==
- A level's causal power depends on two things:
  - **Determinism** — given the current state, how reliably is the next state fixed? (low noise)
  - **Non-degeneracy** — do different states lead to different futures? (not a many-to-one collapse where everything funnels to the same outcome)
- **Coarse-graining can raise both** — average out the noise, lump together the configurations that don't matter — so the macro level predicts its own future *more cleanly* than the micro does. When macro causal power exceeds micro, the higher level is the better place to locate cause and effect.
- **This is exactly the Φʳ the self-scaling-players paper used** ([6 - The Coupling Spine - From the Formal Model to the Authored Top](6-the-coupling-spine-from-the-formal-model-to-the-authored-top.md)): does the merged whole carry information about its own future *beyond* what the parts carry about theirs?
- ==Positive Φʳ = the collective became the better place to locate cause and effect = a real higher-level individual formed== — not just a pile. This is the operational, measurable version of the "we→I" claim in [1 - Organic Alignment Thesis](1-organic-alignment-thesis.md).

## The Free Energy Principle — the *what-next*

- FEP starts from a different question: not "did a new level form?" but ==*what must be true of anything that persists?*==
- The answer: to keep existing, a thing must keep **itself in its expected, characteristic states** — resisting dispersal into all the states it *could* fall into (resisting entropy). A fish persists by staying in water-states, not desert-states.
- **Variational free energy** = a tractable stand-in for *surprise* — how far the system's current state is from what its own model expects. Minimizing free energy ≈ minimizing surprise ≈ staying in expected states. (The "free energy" name is borrowed by analogy from physics; the point is that it is *computable*, since raw surprise can't be evaluated directly.)
- ==**Setpoints as predictions** — the states a system returns to after a perturbation *are* its predictions/preferences, and error-correction toward them *is* free-energy minimization.== This is the "setpoint" language of [2 - The Biological Theory - Transitions & Levin](2-the-biological-theory-transitions-levin.md) and [3 - Coordination Mechanics - Arbitration, Variety & the Channel](3-coordination-mechanics-arbitration-variety-the-channel.md), restated: a homeostatic setpoint is a prediction the system acts to fulfill.

## How the two fit together

- **Causal emergence answers *when*** a self forms → Φʳ > 0 means the whole is a real causal agent.
- **FEP answers *what it then does and why it lasts*** → it minimizes free energy, so it holds its setpoints, resists entropy, and persists. The emergent agents that persist are exactly the ones that minimize free energy.
- **Emergence = the formation of a new FEP-agent one level up.** Once parts couple tightly enough to become a new self (Φʳ > 0), that self gets its own boundary and can be described as minimizing *its own* free energy at *its own* scale.
- Together the two measures capture: ==**"a coherent self exists and is surviving"**== — Φʳ says a higher individual formed; free-energy minimization says it is viable.

## A candidate definition of alignment

- The alignment question, made precise as the **cross-level coupling between the part's free-energy gradient and the whole's:**
- ==Does the part minimize its free energy by *helping the whole minimize its* — or at the whole's *expense*?==
- **Aligned** = the part's path to its own viability runs *through* the whole's (the gradients point the same way). **Misaligned** = the part reduces its own surprise at the whole's expense — the cancer picture: the cell persists by defecting.
- This is the **shared-fate spine of [6 - The Coupling Spine - From the Formal Model to the Authored Top](6-the-coupling-spine-from-the-formal-model-to-the-authored-top.md), in FEP terms:** coupling is aligned when descending your own free-energy gradient also descends the whole's.

## Open questions

- Is this FEP definition equivalent to, or richer than, the "bound, consequence-carrying signal" thesis of [6 - The Coupling Spine - From the Formal Model to the Authored Top](6-the-coupling-spine-from-the-formal-model-to-the-authored-top.md)? Both say a part is aligned when its own viability is only reachable through the whole's.
- **The load-bearing worry:** "minimize free energy" may quietly re-import *persistence* as the top objective — in which case FEP inherits the same normativity gap (4 - Society - Goals & the Economy): it explains *viability*, not *what's worth being viable for*. The empty top goal of [6 - The Coupling Spine - From the Formal Model to the Authored Top](6-the-coupling-spine-from-the-formal-model-to-the-authored-top.md) would still be empty.
- Map the seismology null case (5 - Criticality & the Coordination Spectrum) onto these terms — a stress field is a shared medium with *no setpoint*, i.e. no predictions to minimize surprise against, hence cascade rather than convergence.
